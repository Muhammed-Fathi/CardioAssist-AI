## Adjudication, not activation

- **S-9:** DM, HTN, Current Smoker, EX-Smoker, FH, Typical Chest Pain: numeric 0/1 **representation** locally audited; provisional 0=absent, 1=present direction supported only by secondary Table 1. No owner codebook found. No recoding or change to dictionary 1.1.0; obtain owner codebook/author clarification or a separately scoped, explicitly reviewed scientific decision before treating direction as established.
- **BP / PR:** BP is a single numeric value registered as mmHg; **systolic vs diastolic vs other, posture/resting conditions, device, timing and operator unknown**. PR is numeric registered as beats/minute; some secondary descriptions use “ppm”; pulse-rate intention is plausible, **unit notation, device, conditions and timing unverified**. Source owner/collection form needed. Ranges do not settle semantics.
- **Chest pain:** Typical (numeric) versus Atypical/Nonanginal/Exertional CP (textual) remain separate as delivered. Mutually exclusive vs overlapping elicitation and exertional relationship unknown. Do not collapse, force exclusivity or interpret contradictory patterns as proof. Symptoms may be documented retrospectively after cath.
- **U-6:** No timestamped field-level source documentation establishing the pre-angiography decision moment. Demographics/history are generally obtainable before catheterization, but retrospective recording and post-result coding cannot be excluded. Demand owner acquisition protocol/data dictionary or separately reviewed bounded policy with explicit limitation; do not mark any field VERIFIED from plausibility alone.
- **Target/source independence:** UCI-412's ≥50% angiographic CAD is cross-sectional, not 30-day risk. UCI-45 Cleveland/Hungary documentation uses >50%; exact-boundary equivalence unresolved. UCI-411 extends the same 303 patients, cannot count as independent or additive samples. No cross-source comparison or new evaluation performed.
- **Status and blockers:** All 16 are below VERIFIED, six binary directions S-9 unresolved, BP/PR semantics and chest-pain relation unknown, U-6 all fields, and target threshold H-01 unresolved for harmonization. D-046 read-only audit cannot authorize fitting. D-048 remains proposed in checked-in governance; local M3a signed manifest/provenance/protected source and exact signed protocol bindings must be independently checked by the existing Windows preflight. **A scientific register cannot override any of those gates.** No real-data fitting, clinical scoring or approval is implied by AI-8R engineering.
