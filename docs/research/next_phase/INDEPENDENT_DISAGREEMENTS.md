# Independent disagreements and corrections

2026-11-17; recommendation only. These do **not** edit or supersede the master plan, frozen protocols or decisions. Supporting references S1–S14 and evidence limits are in the [independent review](INDEPENDENT_MEDICAL_ENGINEERING_RESEARCH.md); practical slices are [here](AI9_AI12_EXECUTION_SLICES.md).

1. **AngioCAD is not simply an imaging-only dataset.** Issue #76 calls it post-angiography imaging and says it is not known suitable for pre-test tabular prediction. The second part is correct; the first would be misleading if treated as excluding its clinical table. Its primary 2026 paper [S8] explicitly reports demographic/history/lab data as well as ICA videos for 413 people. Retain it as a potentially valuable **metadata-only** candidate, but timing, missingness, precise eligibility, license and binary target remain unverified. Invasive images remain prohibited inputs for the pre-test task. P25 is supervisor-rejected red; this dataset-suitability review does not promote the paper into selected scope.
2. **Five Cleveland inputs are not proof of a valid CCS workflow.** The preliminary recommendation treats the historical route as useful; agree only as a *bounded historical baseline*. Merely calling a measurement resting does not prove when it was acquired, that cases had stable symptoms or that the cohort represents all suspected CCS. Dataset documentation [S1] is not a per-person timeline. Re-examine source support and avoid a deployable or population-wide assertion. This uncertainty does not block the synthetic engineering path.
3. **No forced same-endpoint pooling.** UCI-412 says ≥50% and UCI-45 says >50% [S1,S2]. Equality is unresolved, not a trivial coding issue; reference methods and population may also differ. Do not call an independent-site evaluation equivalent merely because column names line up. Report a prespecified source-specific target and uncertain compatibility. UCI-411 is the *same* cohort, not a second site [S3,S7]. Statlog is similar, but not conclusively identical; remain overlap-only rather than asserting duplication.
4. **A model with 'calibrated' in its pipeline is not established calibrated for a new population.** The existing calibration code can compute transformations, and synthetic tests demonstrate engineering. Neither a calibration artifact nor an apparent development fit proves good calibrated probabilities for patients; calibration-in-the-large, slope, plots and uncertainty require suitable independent data [S12]. An extrapolated prior-shift adjustment is only a diagnostic assumption. Expose raw and calibration evidence status separately and allow unavailable.
5. **AI-9 labs are not automatically a better model.** Additional tests can carry selection, timing and missingness bias, increase model complexity and create costs. A matched clinical-only vs labs study with paired, fold-contained evaluation is needed; no-go until timing and permissions established. No size rule or five inputs alone guarantees adequate precision [S11].
6. **RF-CL is not a drop-in free baseline.** The 2024 ESC guideline endorses a likelihood framework in a clinical setting [S4], but five Cleveland inputs do not establish all required comparator variables or rights. ESC's software licensing warning covers content included, cited or transformed in software. Scientific citation in this document does not grant algorithm reuse. Use an intercept baseline now; assess original algorithm rights and applicability separately.
7. **Milestone order should not stall independent engineering.** Master plan §17 historically ranks AI-10/11/12 after earlier work and has older AI-8R planning rows despite newer §12.2/v1.7 progress records. Read rows as historical snapshots, not current authorization or a dependency requiring real labs or clinical approval first. Prioritize AI-11 synthetic facade and AI-12 demo concurrently; maintain source holds and gating. AI-8R was accepted on Windows from two disjoint suites, not a completed monolithic suite. No silent rewrite of those historical records.
8. **Complex explanation ≠ trustworthy medical explanation.** The master plan's SHAP/LIME engineering has explicit numerical checks, but faithfulness is scale-, reference-, distribution- and local-surrogate-dependent and neither is causal clinical reasoning. Synthetic mocked backend SHAP-shaped offsets are not genuine SHAP. Expose uncertainty/fidelity or refuse; no personal advice. TRIPOD+AI is a reporting guideline and PROBAST+AI a structured quality/applicability appraisal [S13,S14], not substitutes for evidence.
9. **Don't overclaim endpoint or source evidence.** The worksheet/slides may discuss severity, novelty and individual risk; the selected research target is binary cross-sectional source-defined stenosis, not severity staging, 30-day MI or treatment recommendation. High published accuracy from selected datasets is not independent proof. Selected 216/303 UCI-412 positive frequency [S7] is a selected-cohort fraction, not background prevalence. Clinical relevance and participant labels warrant separate review [S4].
10. **Real evaluation is distinct from deployment approval.** Data-source/authorization requirements are indispensable for protected analyses, but prospective hospital testing, certification and clinical sign-off need not precede completion of an explicitly synthetic academic E2E. Existing D-047 narrow local authorization cannot be generalized to UCI-412, Hungary, new fits or real inference; D-048 and G3/A3 remain unratified/not activated. Synthetic completion is not a scientific validation claim.
