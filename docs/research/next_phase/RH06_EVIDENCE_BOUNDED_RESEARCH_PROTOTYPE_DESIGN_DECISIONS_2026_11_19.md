## Medically meaningful boundaries chosen for the research task

- **Observation and label:** This is a historical *angiography-referenced* binary classification investigation within the dataset's referred population. It is **not** a standardized clinical CAD probability calculator, acute chest-pain assessment or an estimator of next-month events. Do not borrow AHA/ESC likelihood tables, score coefficients, triage algorithms or risk bands as if they were measured by the CardioAssist model. Some professional guideline texts have separate licensing restrictions: cite the high-level literature but do not copy a proprietary calculator into source code.
- **Sources:** UCI-45 includes `chol` and `fbs` as original codebook variables but D-047 used **five** fields; `ca` is angiography-related and excluded from the proposed pre-reference panel. UCI-412 CAD label uses `>=50%`; its lab/echo and 16-field pre-reference chronology require evidence not actually contained in an individual-level timestamp register. No clinical imputation, invented units or target equivalence.
- **Model science and explainability:** Preserve fold-contained preprocessing, inner hyperparameter selection, nested outer evaluation and source-specific calibration as originally recorded. Report discrimination, calibration, uncertainty and sample-size limitations with their evidence grades. SHAP/LIME explanations are associated with a **specific model/scale/background** and **do not establish causal effect**. Do not turn a calibrated synthetic research score into an individual patient's CAD probability.
- **Privacy & safety engineering:** All fixture participants and values are generated; synthetic signing keys and bundles are temporary/locally secured. The static frontend handoff shows labels, rules and errors from safe allowlists only. It never accepts real patient identifiers, exposes submitted feature values, or silently escalates a route.
