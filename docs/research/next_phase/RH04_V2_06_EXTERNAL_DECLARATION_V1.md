For an input link value `z`, define `eta_cal = a + b*z` and `s_cal = expit(eta_cal) = 1/(1+exp(-eta_cal))`. `calibrated_log_odds` is the affine map, and `calibrated_research_score` is its sigmoid image, not an affine map. Neither is a validated clinical probability or future-event estimate. The raw model probability is separate. For the linear route `z` is the exact raw log-odds; for the tree route `z` is the logit of the clipped raw probability (the registered clipping interval is [1e-6, 1-1e-6]). No automatic equivalence between the two routes or cohorts.

The registered OOF calibrator minimizes mean binary logistic loss plus `0.5 * lambda * (a^2 + (b-1)^2)` on development out-of-fold predictions and corresponding labels, not directly on the design matrix. Default `lambda = 1e-3`, bounds `a in [-15,15]`, `b in [0.01,15]`. For the nonconstant fitted path the registered default solver is `scipy_lbfgsb`, with `x0=(0,1)`, analytic gradient and library-default stopping settings. In `ai/calibration.py:756-762` no explicit `tol`, `gtol` or `ftol` is supplied; do not describe `tol=1e-5` as a passed argument.
