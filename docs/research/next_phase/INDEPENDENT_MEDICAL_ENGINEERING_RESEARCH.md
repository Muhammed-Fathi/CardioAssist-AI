## 1. Target and intended use

**Proposed estimand:** among people referred for coronary angiography for suspected chronic coronary syndrome (CCS), at a specified *pre-reference-test* assessment, estimate presence of **source-defined angiographic obstructive epicardial CAD**. UCI-45's 0 versus 1–4 is documented as <50% versus >50% diameter narrowing; UCI-412 defines CAD ≥50% [S1,S2]. Exact 50% and source reference-test interpretation must not be silently harmonized. The data do not prove all subjects had stable symptoms: restrict claims to the documented referred cohort unless ascertainment is established. Angiographic stenosis is not the full syndrome: ANOCA/INOCA exists [S4]. This is not incident 30-day MI, ACS triage, a general-population screen, a diagnosis, therapy or a referral rule.

**Medical rationale:** age, symptoms, history and resting assessment are plausible pre-test inputs; the ESC guideline discusses risk-factor-weighted clinical likelihood in symptomatic suspected CCS [S4]. A dataset of people selected for angiography has referral/verification and spectrum bias; probabilities and predictive values reflect this selected cohort, not unselected primary care. Cross-site referral, case mix, measurement and prevalence differences can change calibration even if ranking survives. Absence of angiographic obstruction does not exclude ischemia. Prevalence-shift correction alone cannot establish transportability; no empirical target prevalence is assumed here. No threshold or clinical action is authorized.

## 4. Ranked options and model design

1. **A — recommended:** existing source-specific Cleveland five-field historical baseline (`age`, `sex`, `cp`, `trestbps`, `restecg`) plus strictly synthetic offline end-to-end system now. Conditional frozen independent Hungary evaluation later. **Limit:** Cleveland's own pre-test chronology and stable-referral eligibility still need source evidence; it is not proven an eligible prospective CCS model. Prior local exploratory results are not independently verified here and cannot be reused as evidence of clinical validity. D-047 governs the historical route and cannot be expanded by this recommendation.
2. **C — independent sources, particularly AngioCAD metadata:** potentially broader clinical/lab data; uncertain timestamps, inclusion, endpoint, availability/license and sample size. Review before any access. Do not add images to a pre-angiography model. This may outperform A scientifically *if* source evidence is obtained, but that is not established.
3. **B — UCI-412:** valuable independent source-specific benchmark with 303 patients but all panel timestamps unverified and specific semantics unresolved; no direct clinical input mapping or real fit until evidence and separate governance. UCI-411 is not new external evidence. If A or C cannot satisfy study conditions, ship an honestly synthetic demo rather than claim a validated real model.

Conditional future model: intercept/prevalence baseline and **penalized logistic regression** with prespecified variables, categorical reference coding, and training-fold imputation/scaling. It is simple and inspectable but can miss nonlinear associations and still overfit. A modest constrained boosting/tree baseline is a secondary hypothesis, only if sample-size budget and protocol allow, not an automatic winner; tuning raises optimism. Training-fold-calibrated logistic or post-hoc calibration on independent cross-fitted predictions is a candidate, not proof of calibration. Compare models on identical outer patient folds; include missingness handling and no target-derived features. Exact RF-CL is not computable from Cleveland's five fields alone, and the ESC explicitly says software use involving guideline content requires a formal license [S4]; cite concept only unless independent rights and inputs are established. A non-ESC intercept-only comparator is immediately implementable synthetically. No assumed risk threshold: abstain for invalid, missing required, out-of-contract, unknown model/version, unverifiable provenance, unapproved source, or unsupported calibration; don't force a score.

**Sample size:** Riley et al. [S11] require anticipated event fraction, candidate parameter count (including dummy variables, nonlinear terms and tuning complexity), expected model fit and desired shrinkage/precision. N=303 alone does not justify 5 or 16 predictors, a 55-variable search or a split; 216/303 is UCI-412's selected outcome fraction, not Cleveland's. Calculate planned N with transparent assumptions and conduct sensitivity analyses before approval; report parameters vs degrees of freedom and effective training size per fold. No numeric adequacy conclusion is possible without the other inputs. External precision/sample size needs separate planning [S12]; Hungary's 294 subjects do not guarantee adequate calibration or subgroup precision.

## 5. Prespecified analysis (proposal; not an authorization)

Use patient-level nested stratified CV for development when allowed: outer folds estimate whole pipeline, inner folds select penalty and any model/calibrator; refit preprocessing, imputation, scaling, feature selection, class weighting and calibration **inside** appropriate folds only. No row leakage, duplicate patient across folds or test-driven choice. Repeated CV can reveal instability but repeats are not independent patients; use a patient-level bootstrap or a justified cross-validation inference method, report uncertainty and small-sample caveats. Compare labs vs clinical on the *same eligible source and same patients/folds* with a prespecified missingness estimand, paired CIs, cost/availability considerations, and full model-complexity budget. Explicit **AI-9 NO-GO** until labs measured before index time and outcome; no imputed speculative source timestamps.

Frozen-model AI-10 plan: prespecify exact model hash, feature mapping, target, endpoint/tie rule, exclusion/missingness, a locked external cohort, calculation/precision target, ledger and report template *before* access; no tuning or recalibration on test outcomes. Distinguish later separately authorized recalibration from original external validation. Report AUROC with CI; AUPRC with prevalence baseline where imbalance matters; Brier score, calibration-in-the-large (fixed slope/intercept), calibration intercept and slope, flexible calibration plot and uncertainty [S12]. Report sensitivity, specificity, PPV and NPV only at **predeclared and justified** thresholds; with no justified thresholds, omit decision classifications. Decision curve / net benefit only if a defensible decision context and threshold range and adequate data are established; otherwise not applicable. Prespecify subgroup contrasts, counts, intervals and missingness/site drift; avoid spurious fairness claims in tiny subgroups. Label indeterminate evidence as such, not failed or validated solely by a point estimate.

Explainability describes model behavior: SHAP depends on background, feature dependence and scale; LIME is a local surrogate requiring fidelity/stability checks. Neither is causal, diagnostic or medical advice. Report output scale, reference/background provenance, version, stability and numerical quality, or return unavailable. Background data may be patient-derived and sensitive. TRIPOD+AI reporting [S13] and PROBAST+AI appraisal [S14] are distinct: good reporting does not erase risk of bias.
