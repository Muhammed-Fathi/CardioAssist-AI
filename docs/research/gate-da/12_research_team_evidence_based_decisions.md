## Our operating agreement

We **do not stop ordinary literature review, reproducible technical audits, source verification, anomaly characterization, decision-matrix authoring, synthetic testing, or documentation** whenever we find uncertainty. We proceed independently within existing authorization, choosing the most defensible **working research recommendation**, documenting alternatives, evidence limits, and what would falsify our hypothesis. The supervisor receives a consolidated package and adjudicates **true clinical judgments/protocol authorization** only. After approval, a frozen protocol governs any model fitting, tuning, feature selection, calibration, SHAP/LIME, clinical evaluation, or publication claims.

**Evidence ranking:** (A) official primary source and byte-verified local aggregate results, (B) independent secondary treatment of this same dataset, (C) team inference or proposed methodology. No primary-source claim is manufactured when the original dictionary is silent.

## Primary/secondary evidence and strength

1. **Primary — original UCI source codebook:** https://archive.ics.uci.edu/ml/machine-learning-databases/heart-disease/heart-disease.names (four collecting sites; two representations; raw 0–4 class counts; cholesterol mg/dl; exercise ST-depression; angiography and missing -9). Codebook **does not expressly establish that cholesterol zero is a missing-value code**. Original archive WARNING: https://archive.ics.uci.edu/ml/machine-learning-databases/heart-disease/WARNING. UCI DOI/license: https://doi.org/10.24432/C52P4X.
2. **Real local original-file evidence:** Cumulative protected audit, aggregated results §§9–14 of [10](10_m2a_uci412_uci411_evidence_and_decisions.md). Source files, raw hashes, manifests unchanged; no individual row included in Git.
3. **Secondary (explicitly not official UCI documentation):** https://rpkgs.datanovia.com/datarium/reference/heartdisease.html describes the **172 zero-cholesterol entries** (123 Switzerland + 49 VA) as not measured; https://ehrdata.readthedocs.io/en/latest/api/data/ehrdata.dt.heart_disease.html treats physiologically impossible zero cholesterol or resting BP as missing. These support the **proposed interpretation**, but cannot prove original collection protocols.
4. **Reporting methodology:** Collins et al., TRIPOD+AI (BMJ 2024), https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj-2023-078378, emphasizes transparent missing-data handling, site heterogeneity, predictor timing, and genuinely nonoverlapping evaluation samples; it **does not authorize model fitting or dictate a specific imputation method**.
