## 1. Decision in one page — recommendation, not approval

**Working question:** at a specified *pre-angiography* assessment of adults already referred for coronary evaluation, can clinician-verified, already-observed information distinguish *contemporaneous, source-defined angiographic CAD presence*? This is a **retrospective feasibility question in referred historical cohorts**, not general-population screening, 30-day event risk, clinical diagnosis or an individual risk percentage. The exact target, reference-standard threshold, population, acquisition moment and formal evaluation protocol are **not frozen** (P-1). UCI-45 says `<50%` for `num=0`, `>50%` for positive; UCI-412/411 says `≥50%` for CAD; the existence/coding of exactly-50% cases is unknown [UCI-45](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/45/heart+disease), [UCI-412](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/412/z+alizadeh+sani), [Gate DA H-01](../gate-da/03_leakage_and_target_harmonization_register.md).

**Recommended research strategy:** retain **two separate, source-specific development candidates**: processed UCI-45 Cleveland for a constrained resting-assessment feasibility question, and UCI-412 for its independently defined basic-clinical-panel question. These are *candidate roles*, not permission to revise either frozen internal benchmark or execute a new one. Do **not** pool, compare their internal AUROCs to choose a dataset, or describe them as mutually externally validated. Preserve UCI-45 Hungary as the **first conditional, untouched site-shift assessment candidate** for a *future locked Cleveland model*; VA and Switzerland remain progressively more constrained candidates. For a future UCI-412 model, an eligible independent UCI-45 source would require a separate **cross-family** outcome/feature mapping; it is not an automatic reciprocal test. There is **no independent-assessment dataset ready today**. It is acceptable to report only limited internal research or to stop if compatibility/precision fail. This refines the *working priority* in the older [Gate DA ranking](../gate-da/02_candidate_ranking.md) in light of subsequent real audits and [Gate PT 06/07/11](06_m2e2_dataset_compatibility_and_independent_assessment_READINESS_REVIEW_DRAFT.md); it does not revoke that record or select a final dataset.

**Evidence levels used below:** **L** = maintainer-executed, authorized local *aggregate* read-only audit reported in merged documentation (not re-run here; originals and protected reports not seen); **M** = maintainer-supplied sanitized *internal experimental* aggregates, not independently reproduced here; **S** = official source metadata/codebook only, not a real-data audit; **X** = unexamined/inaccessible or provenance unresolved. Source metadata can establish listed definitions and licence, **not** record-level coding, participant independence or clinical chronology. The [Phase 0 evidence taxonomy](../../phase0/PHASE0_REVIEW_DRAFT.md) and [Gate PT 11 §0](11_two_dataset_scientific_evidence_synthesis_REVIEW_DRAFT.md) remain controlling. Historical proposed/not-authorized statements in older documents are snapshots, not proof that later local audits/runs did not occur.

## 3. Dataset-to-clinical-workflow compatibility

**Proposed moment W0:** after structured history and *actually performed* resting measurements are available, **before** invasive angiography and before asking for optional labs/stress imaging merely for the model. A W1 (after existing pre-Cath labs) or W2 (after already-indicated exercise testing) model is a **different** workflow/eligibility population. The source records do not prove every measurement preceded catheterization; W0 is a *hypothesis*, not an approved clinical timepoint. [DA 14](../gate-da/14_real_joint_availability_and_staged_intake_decision.md), [TRIPOD+AI](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11019967/) (predictor availability and missing-data reporting).
